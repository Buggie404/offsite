AFTER RECALCULATION

Archetype & reversals

01Find the closest active archetype

The system compares the updated five-axis radar with each active archetype signature by Euclidean distance.

02Check the change conditions

The candidate must be at least {{ data.behavior_rules.archetype_margin }} distance points closer than the current archetype, and {{ data.behavior_rules.archetype_cooldown_days }} days must have passed since the last change. If the current archetype is no longer active, the closest active one is used.

03Reverse or correct evidence

Unsave reverses its saved-item evidence. A full refund reverses the order; a partial refund recalculates the remaining order signal. The radar and archetype are then recalculated.

04Retake the quiz

A new quiz result starts a new baseline. Earlier behavior remains in history but does not affect the new radar.
