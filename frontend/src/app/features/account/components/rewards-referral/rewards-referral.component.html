Referral program

Share your code with friends. You earn points at two steps, and your friend gets a welcome voucher.

1 · Your friend signs up
  • You get +20 points, added to your balance right away.
  • Counts only when they create an account with email or phone and enter your code. Sign-ups with Google or Facebook do not count.
2 · Their first order
  • Your friend gets a WELCOME5 voucher: $5 off their order total.
  • You get +20 points, posted {{ referralHoldDays }} days after their first order is delivered, so cancelled or returned orders don't count.
  • Referral points are not multiplied by your tier.
Rules
  • Your code looks like NAME_AB12CD34. It never expires and can be shared as often as you like.
  • Friends can only enter a code when they sign up, and each account can have one referrer.
  • You can earn rewards for up to 10 friends per year.
  • No first-order reward if the delivery phone number matches your account's phone number.
