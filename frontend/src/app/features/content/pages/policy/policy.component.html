POLICIES

1. Shipping Policy

Standard Shipping Times

We process all orders within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping within the continental US typically takes 3-5 business days. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order has dispatched.

Shipping Rates

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $150. For orders under $150, a flat rate of $9.95 applies for standard shipping. Expedited options are available at checkout.

International Shipping

We currently ship to select international destinations. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Please note that customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

2. Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Return Window

If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange. Items must be initiated for return within this timeframe.

Condition of Items

To be eligible for a return, items must be in their original, unworn, and unwashed condition, with all tags attached. We reserve the right to refuse returns that do not meet these criteria.

Return Process

Visit our returns portal to generate a pre-paid return label. A $5 restocking fee will be deducted from your refund for return shipping. Exchanges are always free.

3. Privacy Policy

Data Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, place an order, or contact customer support. This may include your name, email, shipping address, and payment details.

Usage of Information

We use the information we collect to process your transactions, communicate with you about your order, improve our services, and send promotional communications if you have opted in.

Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
